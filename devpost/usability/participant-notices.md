# Action Receipt trial notices

These notices are entirely invented. No actual school event, payment or deadline is represented.

For your assigned notice, use Action Receipt to prepare a checklist of the actions that apply and a calendar file for dates you can establish from the text. Preserve uncertainty. Stop when you would be comfortable relying on your reviewed files, or when you feel stuck. Describe confusing behavior in your own words.
